## The three separately authorised zinc claims (verbatim, Croatian)
| Claim (annex entry) | Official Croatian wording |
|---|---|
| hair | **Cink doprinosi održavanju normalne kose** |
| nails | **Cink doprinosi održavanju normalnih noktiju** |
| skin | **Cink doprinosi održavanju normalne kože** |

Condition of use printed with each row: *"Tvrdnja se smije koristiti samo za hranu koja je izvor najmanje one količine cinka koja je navedena u tvrdnji IZVOR [NAZIV VITAMINA] I/ILI [NAZIV MINERALA] iz Priloga Uredbi (EZ) br. 1924/2006."* (= at least a *source of zinc*: ≥ 15 % of the reference value per portion). The product page states **10 mg zinc per 8 g serving**, which is 100 % of the reference value (10 mg) — the condition is met on the page's figures.

## What the slide says (v4) and how it relates
Slide 5 v4: **"Cink doprinosi održavanju normalne kože i kose te normalnih noktiju."** This combines the three authorised statements into one sentence, keeping each noun in the case required by the official wording ("normalne kože", "kose" (as in "normalne kose"), "normalnih noktiju"). It is the same meaning as the three claims; whether combining them into one sentence is acceptable for a given regulator is a **legal question for a human**. The 7E wording ("…normalne kože, kose i noktiju.") put hair and nails into a genitive series that does not match the official case forms, which is why it was replaced.

## Required accompanying information (Art. 10(2) of Reg. 1924/2006) — status unchanged
Statement on a varied, balanced diet and healthy lifestyle; the quantity and pattern of consumption needed for the effect; a statement for persons who should avoid the product; a warning against exceeding the daily intake. All four are in the **Post 2 caption** (taken from the product page); slide 4 shows the serving (1 mjerica (8 g), 200–300 ml). Whether they must also appear on a slide is **open (human/legal)**.

## What this does NOT establish
* It does not clear the post: "NAPITAK ZA LJEPOTU" and "Ljepota počinje iznutra." are general, non-specific benefit statements (Art. 10(3)) that sit beside the specific claim; the product is promoted as a *collagen* beauty drink while the authorised claim concerns *zinc*; a reviewer may read an implied collagen benefit.
* Collagen (6300 mg), hyaluronic acid (40 mg) and grape-seed extract (400 mg) have **no** authorised hair/skin/nail claim; none is made on any slide.
* The product page's own text "Za podršku koži, kosi i noktima iznutra …" is **not** reproduced.
* No national supplement-marketing rules were checked.
